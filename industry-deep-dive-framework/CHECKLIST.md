# Deep Dive Completeness Checklist

Use this to grade a deep dive before calling it finished. **Must-haves** are the minimum bar;
a deep dive missing any of them is incomplete. **Strong-to-haves** separate a good deep dive
from a great one.

## Must-haves (the minimum bar)
- [ ] **One-line thesis** stated up front
- [ ] **Why now** — at least 3 concrete reasons it's timely
- [ ] **Plain-English definition** that passes the 5-year-old test
- [ ] **How it's made / works** — a process flow + raw-material cost stack
- [ ] **Segments** broken out (not treated as one monolith)
- [ ] **Value-chain margin map** — where profit pools sit ⭐
- [ ] **Industry structure** — concentrated vs fragmented + barriers to entry
- [ ] **Demand drivers vs supply** + an explicit **cycle-position** call
- [ ] **Evaluation scorecard** defined *before* company analysis ⭐
- [ ] At least **2–3 company deep dives** with **both** Balance Sheet and P&L
- [ ] **Valuation** with a method appropriate to the sector
- [ ] **Dedicated Risks section**
- [ ] **Conclusion / verdict** that is *your own* view
- [ ] **Data sources / how to track** appendix

## Strong-to-haves (good → great)
- [ ] Porter's Five Forces explicitly worked through
- [ ] History told as **eras** (shows how today's structure formed)
- [ ] **Geography**: region-by-region demand + production-vs-consumption split
- [ ] **Policy** landscape mapped (subsidies, duties, licences, resource rights)
- [ ] Players **tiered into groups** (Leaders / Challengers / Laggards) before deep dives
- [ ] **Integration** dynamics noted (who's going backward/forward and why)
- [ ] **Next-gen technology** flagged (where the puck is going)
- [ ] At least one **case study** that makes an abstract point concrete
- [ ] **Triggers** are specific and time-bound (not "things may improve")
- [ ] A **"bubble / margin-of-safety"** sanity check on valuations

## Quality bar (craft, not coverage)
- [ ] **Facts > opinions** — claims backed by numbers/sources, not vibes
- [ ] **Altitude shifts** — moves between macro (why now), mid (structure/value chain),
      and ground (process, single companies). Not stuck at one level.
- [ ] **Comparable company layouts** — every company analysed with the same template
- [ ] **Self-researchable** — a reader could update this themselves from the sources listed
- [ ] **Visual where it counts** — value chain, timeline, Porter's, margin map are diagrams,
      not paragraphs

## Generic data-source starter list
Adapt per industry; this is the default kit observed across the decks.

**Primary / company-level**
- Company annual reports, investor presentations, earnings-call transcripts
- Stock-exchange filings & corporate announcements (BSE/NSE; SEC for US names)
- Capacity / order-book disclosures

**Industry / macro**
- Sector regulator & ministry sites; government policy docs (PLI, duties, NITI Aayog)
- Industry associations & councils (e.g., GWEC for wind, sector cement associations)
- IEA, BloombergNEF, McKinsey / BCG / Bain sector reports
- Goldman Sachs / BlackRock / Nomura sector research
- Specialist analysts & creators on YouTube / X / Substack
- Trade press & data services (Bloomberg, CNBC, Quartz)

**Tracking cadence**
- What to watch monthly/quarterly (prices, utilisation, order wins, capacity additions)
- Which 3–5 "tell" metrics signal the cycle is turning for *this* industry
