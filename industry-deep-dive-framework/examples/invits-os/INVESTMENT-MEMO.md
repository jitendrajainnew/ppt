## THE VARIANT VIEWS — where 80% of the return is

### Variant 1 — asset *type* is everything (and it's mispriced)
- **Consensus:** InvITs = high-yield bond-proxies on "risky infra"; the ~9–13% yield is the whole story (and it's risky).
- **Variant [M]:** **Availability-based transmission (IndiGrid, PowerGrid InvIT) is mispriced as if it
  carried toll-road traffic risk.** ~99% availability + AAA + a wide ~250–600 bps spread means these are
  *contracted* cash flows, not volume bets — and accretive acquisitions off the NMP/grid-capex pipeline
  convert the depreciating-asset problem into a **compounding cash-DPU engine.** *Own availability/annuity;
  the toll trusts are the actual risk.*
- **Evidence:** IndiGrid OPM rock-steady **85–96% FY18–25** with Sales 448→3,288cr (availability quality);
  IRB OPM ~80% but Sales choppy (992→1,442→1,062→1,084→1,549) tracking traffic. [S: Screener.in, ~June 2026]
- **Falsified if:** transmission availability breaches, or accretion spreads turn negative as the TBCB
  pipeline gets crowded.

### Variant 2 — yield ≠ income (headline yield is *inversely* related to durability)
- **Consensus:** higher yield = better income; clip the biggest "coupon" (IRB ~12%+).
- **Variant [H]:** On a **finite-life concession, part of every distribution is return of your own capital.**
  IRB's ~12%+ yield embeds the *most* return-of-capital + traffic risk and its **DPU is falling** (₹10.55→
  ₹8.0→₹6.3–6.5 guide); IndiGrid's lower ~9–10% yield is the most *cash-covered and growing*. **The highest
  headline yield is the lowest-quality income.** Decompose every DPU into interest / dividend / return-of-capital.
- **Evidence:** IRB DPU trajectory + dividend payouts routinely >100% of GAAP profit (structural — NDCF
  includes amortisation); PGInvIT held ₹12 DPU FY25 partly from *reserves*. [S: report Mod 8b/12; Screener.in]
- **Falsified if:** IRB's DPU stabilises on a *cash* (not RoC) basis and traffic re-accelerates.

### Variant 3 — GAAP earnings are the wrong meter (the PGInvIT proof)
- **Consensus:** read InvITs like operating companies — watch EPS/ROCE.
- **Variant [H]:** **EPS/ROCE are noise here.** PGInvIT's net profit went −₹446cr (FY23) → +₹1,172cr (FY25),
  EPS −₹2.14 → ₹12.92, OPM% −24% → 133%, ROCE −6% → 16% — **all on flat ~₹1,260cr revenue and the same
  transmission assets earning the same availability fee.** The swing is concession depreciation/amortisation,
  not cash. *Sell PGInvIT on its FY23 "loss" and you'd have dumped an asset still throwing off stable cash.*
  Value on NDCF/DPU.
- **Evidence:** PGInvIT P&L FY22–26 [S: Screener.in, ~June 2026]; IndiGrid 90%-OPM-yet-8%-ROCE paradox.
- **Falsified if:** an InvIT's GAAP profit and its NDCF/DPU start moving together (they won't on a
  finite-life concession).

## ★ FALSIFICATION TEST — the thesis is proven WRONG if:
(a) **DPU is sustained only by rising leverage or return-of-capital** — NDCF coverage falls below 1x
(watch: PGInvIT's reserve-funding of ₹12 DPU is an early tell); **or**
(b) **acquisitions are done at sub-cost-of-capital yields** — accretion spread turns negative as the TBCB/
NMP pipeline gets competitive; **or**
(c) **a sponsor governance/pledge event or a related-party over-priced drop-down** hits; **or**
(d) **toll-trust traffic shortfalls** (IRB) **or a transmission availability breach** (IndiGrid/PGInvIT)
materialise; **or**
(e) total return collapses to **just the yield minus rate de-rating** with no accretive growth.

## INVESTMENT FIT — a great instrument can still be a bad investment

| Fit | Yes/No | At what price / cycle point? |
|---|---|---|
| **Availability-compounder** | **Yes** — IndiGrid (AAA, cash-DPU growth, accretive) | Buy **at/below NAV** with a **wide, cash-backed yield-spread** |
| **Low-risk bond-proxy** | **Yes** — PowerGrid InvIT (near-unlevered) | For *safety not growth*; demand NDCF covers DPU from cash (not reserves) |
| **Cyclical (rate play)** | **Yes** — duration asset | Buy when the 10Y is peaking / cuts approaching |
| **Traffic-risk yield trap** | **No / avoid** — IRB InvIT (declining DPU, RoC-heavy) | Only on a *visible cash-coverage / traffic inflection*, sized small |
| **Special situation** | Selective — internalisation / NMP drop-down | Event-driven (governance/accretion re-rate), sized small |

## The one-line thesis
**Asset type is destiny on a finite-life concession: own availability-based transmission run by disciplined
accretive acquirers (IndiGrid; PowerGrid InvIT for safety) where the DPU is cash-backed and growing, and
avoid pure traffic-risk toll (IRB) where the high headline yield is partly your own capital returning.
Value on NDCF coverage + DPU sustainability + the yield-vs-G-sec spread — never EPS/ROCE (PGInvIT's
−₹446cr→₹1,172cr swing on flat revenue is the proof).**

*Answers the Research Question: the better Indian InvITs are **not** pure depreciating-asset yield traps —
availability-based, AAA, accretive, low-return-of-capital trusts are total-return compounders mispriced as
if they carried toll risk; but they remain leveraged, rate-sensitive duration assets, so entry on a wide,
cash-backed yield-spread at/below NAV matters, and you must decompose the DPU.* **[M]**
