> on AAA/7.74% debt and 33% NDCF CAGR; PGInvIT is near-unlevered but **funded FY25's ₹12 DPU partly from

> and its GAAP net profit (356) sits far below its distribution. **Interpretation** — headline yield is
> *inversely* related to income durability: IRB's ~12%+ yield embeds the most return-of-capital + traffic
> risk; PGInvIT's reserve-funded payout is not fully cash-covered; IndiGrid's lower headline yield is the
> most *cash-covered and growing*. The OPM/ROCE/Net-Profit rows are shown only to demonstrate they are the
> **wrong meter** — PGInvIT's −₹446cr→₹1,172cr swing on flat revenue proves it. **Conclusion** — IndiGrid is
> the compounder, PGInvIT the low-risk-but-no-growth bond-proxy with a coverage warning, IRB the high-yield
> trap. **Asset type + NDCF coverage > headline yield. [H]**

## What to watch next quarter
1. **NDCF coverage of DPU + DPU composition** (cash vs return-of-capital) per trust — esp. whether PGInvIT
   covers its ₹12 DPU from *cash* (not reserves) and whether IRB confirms the ₹6.3–6.5 FY27 guide.
2. **10Y G-sec vs distribution-yield spread** — a 50–75 bps back-up in the 10Y compresses the spread and
   de-rates NAV (the Very-High-severity thesis-killer).
3. **Net debt/AUM trend** — any creep toward the 70% cap + rating actions (AAA at risk).
4. **Accretion spread on new deals** — asset yield-on-cost vs cost of capital; sub-cost-of-capital deals
   falsify the compounder thesis.
5. **Availability% (transmission) / traffic growth (toll)** — 2 quarters of negative toll traffic vs
   forecast = IRB DPU-miss tell; any availability breach at IndiGrid/PGInvIT.
