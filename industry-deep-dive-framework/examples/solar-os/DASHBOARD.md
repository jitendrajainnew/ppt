**Cross-reads from the 10-yr data [H]:**
- **Module makers swing high:** Waaree ROCE 16→52→39%; Premier 13→4→41→33%. OPM 5%→22% (Waaree), 10%→30% (Premier) — operating leverage shows up as margin *and* ROCE expansion together. **Treat 35–40% ROCE as cyclical-peak, not steady-state.** [verify]
- **Borosil is the live counter-example:** OPM collapsed to 4% (FY24-25), ROCE −4%, two loss years — then OPM 28%, ROCE 25%, profit ₹127cr (FY26). The component closest to commodity glass already lived a full down-cycle → the canary for what over-supply does to manufacturing returns. **[H]**
- **IPP = high-margin, low-ROCE by construction:** Adani Green 57–83% OPM but ROCE only 7–10%; interest (₹6,484cr FY26ttm) nearly equals operating profit's PBT contribution; PBT stayed negative through FY21. Margin says the *asset* is good; ROCE says the *capital structure* eats most of it. **[H]**
- **Tata Power = the anchor:** steady ~11% ROCE, OPM ~21–23%, no swings; the integrated utility averages out the cycle. **[M]**

## What to watch next month
1. **Module ASP + polysilicon price** — has the manufacturing trough bottomed, or does India ASP track China down sharply (thesis-killer)?
2. **ALMM / BCD / DCR notifications** — any relief, exemption list, or PLI-tranche change (the single fastest way to halve a maker).
3. **India module/cell utilisation** — does India fall below ~60% (over-build emerging) while cell remains the bottleneck?
4. **US AD/CVD + 45X status** — export-premium durability for Waaree across any policy change.
5. **Auction winning tariffs (₹/kWh)** — below equity-IRR threshold? + IPP net-debt/interest cover each result.
6. **Borosil-style margin signal** — is any India manufacturing margin starting to compress toward Borosil's FY24-25 trough?
