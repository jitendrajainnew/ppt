## The rubric (weights) — derived from `solar.md` Module 11

Module 11 weights policy resilience, integration, cost/W, demand security, annuity and capital
allocation. Re-expressed here into the six requested solar factors (the chain-position and tech
nodes that the 10-yr data can actually *test*):

| Factor | Weight | "5/5" |
|---|---|---|
| **Value-chain position / integration** | 25% | Poly/wafer→cell→module integrated, or owns the annuity (IPP) — survives a Chinese ASP crash |
| **Margin / ROCE quality** | 20% | High *and* durable returns through-cycle (not a single up-cycle year) |
| **Balance sheet** | 15% | Self-funds capex; low leverage; not interest-eaten |
| **Scale / capacity** | 15% | Multi-GW nameplate (or GW operating for IPP) + bankability |
| **Tech edge (TOPCon / HJT / cell node)** | 15% | At/ahead of current node; cost-down roadmap |
| **Demand visibility / order book** | 10% | DCR cell scarcity, US export book, or contracted PPA pipeline |

**Scale:** 1 red flag · 3 average · 5 best-in-class. **Good = integrated + durable-return + funded + scaled + at-node + demand-contracted.**

## Factor detail (the evidence behind each score)

**Value-chain position / integration (25%)**

- **Waaree → 4:** largest module maker backward-integrating into cells + entering IPP; integration building but module-led today.
- **Borosil → 2:** single-rung **solar-glass component** (~8–12% of module cost); a protected input niche but not integrated — and it *lived* the commodity down-cycle (loss FY24-25).

**Margin / ROCE quality (20%)** — rewards *durability*, penalises single-up-cycle peaks

- **Adani Green → 3:** 83% OPM looks elite but ROCE only 7–10% — interest eats it; *margin good, returns mediocre by construction.*
- **Tata Power → 3:** steady 11% ROCE, 21% OPM — modest but the most durable, no swings.
- **Borosil → 2:** OPM 4% and ROCE −4% in FY24-25; even the FY26 snap-back to 28%/25% is one cycle-turn quarter set, not durable.

**Balance sheet (15%)**

1. **Waaree takes #1 (over Adani Green in solar.md's illustrative table).** The 10-yr data shows
   Waaree compounding *hardest* — ₹1,996cr→₹26,537cr (~13×), OPM 5%→22%, ROCE 16→52→39%, profit
   ₹3,884cr — with the best **US export order book**. On *this* rubric (which weights margin/ROCE
   quality 20% and de-emphasises pure annuity), the hypergrowth high-ROCE module maker wins **now**.
   The catch: those returns are **up-cycle**. **[H on the data; M on durability]**
2. **Adani Green drops to #4 despite the strongest chain position.** Integration scores 5/5 (the
   annuity), but the 10-yr data exposes the leverage: ROCE only 7–10% and interest ₹6,484cr eating
   the 83% OPM. A leveraged yield play, not a high-return compounder — balance sheet 2/5 drags it. **[H]**
3. **Premier is a genuine #2, not the #4 of the illustrative table.** Cell-first integration + ROCE
   to 41% + OPM 30% put it ahead of both utilities on return quality — tempered only by smaller scale
   and the FY22-23 loss years still in the record. **[H]**
4. **Borosil is the clear laggard AND the warning shot.** ROCE −4%, two loss years, then a FY26
   snap-back (OPM 28%, ROCE 25%) — the chain's most commoditised rung living a full down-cycle. It
   is the data's evidence that the makers' high returns are **not proven structural**. **[H]**

## ⚠️ The flag: manufacturing-margin sustainability

Waaree's 22% OPM / 39% ROCE and Premier's 30% / 33% are **up-cycle numbers**, earned while Indian
supply was short and protected (ALMM/BCD/DCR/PLI). **Borosil is the warning from one rung up:** the
component closest to commodity glass already collapsed to −4% ROCE before recovering. **If** India
module capacity over-builds (as global modules already did) **and** duties/PLI ease, manufacturing
margins could compress toward Borosil's FY24-25 trough rather than hold. The scores above price the
*current* margin map; they do **not** assume the makers' high returns are structural — that is the
open question and the #1 re-grade trigger. **[H — the risk is well-evidenced; the outcome is not yet decided]**

## Re-grade triggers
- **Waaree / Premier OPM holds ~20%+ through a module over-build** → margin/ROCE durability confirmed → up-cycle reclassified as structural → scores hold/rise.
- **India module ASP tracks China down + ALMM/BCD ease** → maker margins compress toward Borosil's trough → Waaree/Premier margin 4→2, scores fall sharply.
- **Adani Green de-levers (interest cover improves, ROCE crosses ~12%)** → balance sheet 2→3 → score → ~3.75.
- **Borosil sustains FY26 recovery (anti-dumping protection holds, OPM stays >20%)** → margin 2→3 → score → ~2.55.
