## THE VARIANT VIEWS — where 80% of the return is

### Variant 1 — manufacturing margins are commoditising (not structural)
- **Consensus:** Indian solar manufacturing = a PLI-fuelled secular growth story; pay growth multiples for the module makers.
- **Variant [M]:** The 35–40% ROCE and 20–30% OPM at Waaree/Premier are **up-cycle numbers**, earned while domestic supply was short and protected. Modules are commoditising toward China cost; India's edge is a **policy window, not a cost moat**. *Own the makers as cyclicals sized to the ASP/policy cycle, not as compounders.*
- **Evidence (REAL data):** **Borosil**, one rung up the chain in commodity solar glass, already lived it — OPM collapsed to 4%, ROCE −4%, two loss years (FY24-25), then snapped to 28%/25% (FY26). The component closest to commodity already did a full down-cycle. Global module ASPs fell ~50% in 2023-24. [verify]
- **Falsified if:** India makers reach **China-level cost via full poly-to-module integration**, OR **ALMM/BCD made permanent through ~2035**, OR module **ASPs stabilise above replacement cost**, OR Waaree/Premier OPM holds ~20%+ *through* a module over-build.

### Variant 2 — value migrates DOWN the chain (annuity + integration capture it, not bare assembly)
- **Consensus:** Own "solar manufacturing" broadly; capacity = value.
- **Variant [M]:** Value migrates to whoever owns **the protected factory AND the annuity asset** — or the scarce node (cells). Bare module assembly into a glut destroys value. The data ranks **integration position** as the 25% factor and it sorts the panel: Premier's **cell-first** integration earns 41% ROCE; Borosil's single-rung glass earns −4%.
- **Evidence (REAL data):** Premier ₹947→₹7,824cr (~8×), turned profitable FY24, ROCE to 41% via **cell integration**; Waaree backward-integrating cells + entering IPP. Adani Green owns the annuity (83% OPM, 25-yr PPAs) — durable demand even if low-ROCE.
- **Falsified if:** standalone module-only names sustain maker-level margins without integration (i.e., the glut never reaches India), OR cell scarcity resolves and the DCR premium evaporates.

### Variant 3 — the IPP "annuity premium" is half a leverage trap
- **Consensus:** IPPs are safe annuity compounders — high margins, contracted cashflows.
- **Variant [M-H]:** High OPM ≠ high returns. **Adani Green runs 57–83% OPM but only 7–10% ROCE**; **interest ₹6,484cr (FY26ttm) nearly equals operating profit's contribution to PBT**, and PBT was negative through FY21. The asset is good; the capital structure eats most of it. *Underwrite IPPs on cash yield + balance sheet, not headline OPM — and prefer the integrated utility (Tata Power, steady 11% ROCE) for lower-volatility exposure.*
- **Evidence (REAL data):** Adani Green ROCE 10→8→8→10→9→7%; Tata Power flat ~11% ROCE, OPM 21%, no swings — the "boring middle."
- **Falsified if:** IPP ROCE structurally crosses ~12–15% as rates fall / leverage normalises and tariffs hold.

## ★ FALSIFICATION TEST — the thesis is proven WRONG if (any of):
1. India makers reach **China-level cost via full poly-to-module integration** (Reliance/Adani giga-complexes deliver), OR
2. **ALMM / BCD made permanent through ~2035** (the policy window becomes a permanent wall), OR
3. Module **ASPs stabilise sustainably above replacement cost** — a real through-cycle margin, OR
4. The **US export premium proves durable across an administration change** (AD/CVD + 45X survive), OR
5. **Waaree/Premier OPM holds ~20%+ through an India module over-build** (the Borosil-style compression never comes).

Any one of these makes Indian solar manufacturing a **structural compounder**, not a cyclical
commodity — and flips the makers from "trade the cycle" to "own the compounder."
