## The rubric (weights) — derived from holding-companies.md Module 11  [CORE]

> Module 11 lists 6 criteria (25/25/20/15/10/5). We fuse **Balance sheet (10%) + Liquidity/float (5%)**
> into one **Balance sheet / liquidity (15%)** factor to mirror the pharma-os 5-factor card. Weights
> otherwise unchanged.

| Factor | Weight | "5/5" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Discount depth + catalyst visibility** | 25% | Deep discount (50–70%) **AND** a credible, named catalyst (buyback/demerger/payout step-up) |
| **Underlying asset quality / NAV growth** | 25% | Blue-chip, listed, liquid underlyings compounding NAV double-digit |
| **Payout / buyback policy** | 20% | Buyback-at-discount **+** high/rising payout — the only proven unlock |
| **Governance / promoter intent** | 15% | Clean, no related-party drain, **and** intent to close the discount |
| **Balance sheet / liquidity** | 15% | Net cash, no holdco debt; adequate float to re-rate |
|  | **100%** | |

**Scale:** 1 = red flag · 3 = average · 5 = best-in-class. *Criterion 1 deliberately fuses cheapness
AND catalyst — a holdco scoring 5 on discount but 1 on catalyst is a trap, not a buy.*

## ⛔ Why ROCE is the WRONG metric (read before the scores)  [CORE]

| | Bajaj Holdings | Tata Investment |
|---|---|---|
| **Reported ROCE (FY26ttm)** | 11% (range 9–13%) | 2% (range 1–2% post-FY18) |
| **Look-through Net Profit FY15→FY26ttm** | ₹2,029cr → **₹9,789cr (≈4.8×, ~15% CAGR)** | ₹186cr → **₹434cr (≈2.3×, ~8% CAGR)** |
| **Why ROCE lies** | Numerator = only *recognised* dividend/equity-method income; denominator carries the holdings **at cost/equity value, not market** — unrealised NAV gains excluded → ratio mechanically crushed | Same mechanism, worse: capital employed balloons with the carrying portfolio while only realised income flows through P&L → ROCE collapses to 1–2% despite the book compounding |
| **Correct read** | NAV compounding ~15% — a *good* asset wrongly scored on ROCE | NAV compounding ~8% — *not* a deteriorated franchise |

*Conclusion:* **anyone screening holdcos on ROCE rejects the whole category for the wrong reason.**
Score the NAV, the discount, and the catalyst — never the ROCE. **[H]**

## Scoring reasoning (evidence-led)  [CORE]

### 1 — Bajaj Holdings & Investment · 3.80/5  [H on data, M on catalyst]
- **Disc+Catalyst = 3.** Deep ≈50–55% discount `[verify]` = a real margin of safety **but no buyback / no demerger** — cheapness without a fired catalyst caps this at average.
- **Asset Quality/NAV = 5.** Underlyings are **Bajaj Auto + Bajaj Finserv** — two of India's best compounders; look-through NP **₹2,029→₹9,789cr (~15% CAGR)**, almost all listed & liquid = cleanest NAV in the category.
- **Payout/Buyback = 3.** Dividend yield ≈1.8–3%, rising-ish, DPS ₹130 declared `[verify]` — pays you to wait, **but the defining omission is no buyback** at a ~50% discount (≈2× accretive, refused).
- **Governance = 4.** Conservative, net-cash, no scandal — a good *custodian*; held below 5 because promoter intent is **control + stability, not discount-closing**.
- **Balance Sheet/Liq = 4.** Net cash, no meaningful holdco debt; tiny float / index dynamics cap it short of 5.

### 2 — Tata Investment Corporation · 3.10/5  [H on data, M on catalyst]
- **Disc+Catalyst = 3.** Even deeper ≈55–65% discount `[verify]` ("unusual discount" per ValuePickr) — cheap, but again **no buyback/demerger** catalyst.
- **Asset Quality/NAV = 3.** A **diversified, partly market-linked** book (Tata names + listed securities); look-through NP **₹186→₹434cr (~8% CAGR)** — compounds, but slower and less concentrated-blue-chip than Bajaj's.
- **Payout/Buyback = 2.** Low dividend yield (~0.4–1%) `[verify]`, low–moderate payout, **no buyback** — weakest "paid to wait" of the two.
- **Governance = 4.** Tata-group oversight, clean record; same control-not-minority intent caps it at 4.
- **Balance Sheet/Liq = 4.** Net-cash investment book, no leverage; small float.
