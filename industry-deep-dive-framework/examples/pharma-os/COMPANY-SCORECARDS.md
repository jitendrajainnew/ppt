## ⚠️ What reading the primary filings CHANGED (vs the quick search pass)

1. **Cipla overtook Sun for #1.** The deep read found **all 5 Cipla USFDA sites VAI** + **₹10,369cr net
   cash** → compliance 4→5. Meanwhile Sun's **Halol OAI/import ban + failed Jun-2025 re-inspection** is a
   live negative (compliance 3). *The pristine-compliance compounder edges the bigger-franchise one.* **[H]**
2. **Sun's GLP-1 is earlier-stage than I'd said.** GL0034 is **Phase 1 completed** (filing), not Phase 2 —
   the quick pass overstated it. Corrected.
3. **Dr Reddy's US concentration is starker than "US-heavy."** North America is **~45% of revenue**
   (₹14,516cr) vs India only ~17% — it is *the* most erosion-exposed major. Reinforces franchise = 3.
4. **Divi's is now a CDMO-majority company** — custom synthesis ~56% of mix (Q2FY26), trading ~51× forward
   (Sharekhan PT). Confirms the variant that CDMO *valuations* have run ahead of cash flows.
