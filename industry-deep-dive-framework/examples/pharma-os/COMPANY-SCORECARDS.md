> The asset most reports skip: **same rubric, every company, ranked.** Scores are analyst
> judgment shown *with the evidence* so you can re-grade. Data ~FY25, [S]-sourced. ⚠️ Verify.

## The rubric (weights)

| Factor | Weight | What "5/5" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Compliance** | 25% | Clean USFDA record, no open warning letter / import alert |
| **R&D / pipeline** | 20% | Productive specialty / complex / biosimilar / GLP-1 pipeline |
| **Capital allocation** | 20% | Value-accretive M&A + capex, disciplined, high incremental ROCE |
| **Franchise** | 20% | Sticky domestic brand and/or differentiated specialty/CDMO moat |
| **Balance sheet** | 15% | Net cash / low leverage, self-funds growth |

Scale: 1 = red flag · 3 = average · 5 = best-in-class.

## Why each scored what it did (the reasoning)

- **Sun (4.30):** Best franchise (#1 India + $1.2bn global specialty, 20% of rev) + net cash ($3.1bn)
  + strongest specialty R&D (incl. novel GLP-1 Phase 2). **Held back only by Halol** (OAI/import ban,
  failed Jun-2025 re-inspection) → compliance 3. *The bull case is "Halol is contained because specialty
  drives the value"; the bear case is the 2025 ProPublica/STAT findings.*
- **Divi's (4.20):** Cleanest compliance (5) + debt-free (5) + ROCE recovering to 18.4%. **Capped by no
  domestic franchise and process-R&D (not novel)** → R&D 3. The purest BioSecure/China+1 + peptide-API play.
- **Cipla (4.15):** Balanced — strong One-India franchise (42%), respiratory/peptide R&D, net cash. Goa
  483 keeps compliance at 4. The "no glaring weakness" compounder.
- **Torrent (3.85):** Best margins (31.4%) + ROCE (24.3%) + sticky branded franchise (5), **but leverage**
  (acquisition history) → balance sheet 3 and modest R&D.
- **Dr Reddy's (3.75):** Solid balance sheet + R&D, **but most US-generic-erosion exposure** (franchise 3)
  and the debated NRT consumer acquisition (cap-alloc 3).
- **Mankind (3.65):** Best domestic franchise (4.8% share, consumer brands) + 27% ROCE, **but the ₹13,768cr
  debt-funded BSV deal** raised leverage (finance costs up, PAT fell) → balance sheet 3, cap-alloc 3 (TBD).

## How to use this
- **Re-grade quarterly** as compliance status / margins / leverage change (e.g., a Halol clearance lifts
  Sun's compliance 3→4 → score → ~4.55, likely #1 by a wide margin).
- Cross-reference each score against the **Red Flag Checklist** (pharma.md Module 5c) before sizing.
