## THE VARIANT VIEWS — where 80% of the return is

### Variant 1 — "US generics have bottomed" is half-wrong (and that's the opportunity)
- **Consensus:** Indian pharma is a cheap generics trade; pricing is bottoming.
- **Variant [M]:** Erosion is **still high-single-digit in commodity oral baskets in 2026** — the bottom
  call is *premature for plain generics*. The real money is the **mix-shift to specialty/complex/CDMO**,
  not a generic-pricing recovery. *Own the mix-shifters (Sun, Divi's, Cipla); avoid single-plant commodity-oral names.*
- **Evidence:** BusinessToday/Business Standard (Jan-2026): pricing pressure "little sign of easing in
  2026," capital redirecting to oncology/obesity/auto-immune.
- **Falsified if:** crowded-basket erosion drops to low-single-digit *and* specialty mix stalls.

### Variant 2 — GLP-1 winners aren't only the innovators
- **Consensus:** Novo/Lilly capture GLP-1 value; generics are an afterthought.
- **Variant [M-H]:** **Indian generic semaglutide is live now** (patent expired Mar-2026; Dr Reddy's
  "Obeda" launched) and the **peptide-API/CDMO suppliers (Divi's) capture more value than the market
  credits** — domestic ₹5,000cr by 2030 + a potential **~$20bn export** wave at ~10–15% of brand price.
- **Evidence:** GrandView/CareEdge/Kotak sizing; Mar-2026 launches.
- **Falsified if:** generic GLP-1 pricing collapses faster than volume scales, or regulatory/manufacturing
  (injectable-pen) barriers throttle the Indian ramp.

### Variant 3 — the CDMO rerating may be ahead of the cash flows (contrarian-to-the-hype)
- **Consensus:** BioSecure + China+1 = secular CDMO re-rating; pay up (P/E >50x).
- **Variant [M]:** RFPs are rising but **Syngene already shows margin compression / capacity under-
  absorption** — orders haven't fully converted. The *structural story is real, the valuation prices in
  flawless execution.* Prefer the **cash-backed compounder (Divi's, ROCE 18.4% and rising)** over the
  story stocks; demand order-book proof before paying 50x.
- **Falsified if:** order-book/utilisation inflects up sharply over 2–3 quarters (then the multiple is earned).

### Variant 4 — Sun's Halol overhang is mispriced *both ways*
- **Consensus:** "Halol is a manageable overhang."
- **Variant [M]:** The 2025 ProPublica/STAT findings + failed Jun-2025 re-inspection + unannounced-
  inspection regime make the *tail risk* worse than consensus — **but** Halol is a small slice of Sun's
  specialty-driven value, so the *stock impact is contained*. The real compliance landmine is in **single-
  plant commodity-generic names**, not Sun. *Net: Sun's risk is overstated at the franchise level, the
  sector's compliance risk is understated at the commodity-name level.*

## The one-line thesis
**Value is migrating from commodity US generics to domestic-branded + CDMO + GLP-1/specialty; own the
integrated mix-shifters with clean compliance (Sun ex-Halol-risk, Divi's, Cipla), demand order-book proof
before paying CDMO hype multiples, and treat single-plant commodity-oral names as cheap-for-a-reason.**

*Answers the Research Question: yes, the value migration is real and the GLP-1 wave is a genuine
multi-year volume driver — but the CDMO *valuation* has run ahead of the *cash flows*.*
