### What the company table says
- **IEX = the purest toll-road:** **84% OPM held flat for five straight years (FY22→FY26ttm)**, ROCE 50–61% — the most *stable* margin in the set. Stability, not peak, is the moat tell. **[H]**
- **CDSL = the cyclicality warning:** sales still grew (1,082→1,145) but **net profit FELL 526→455 and ROCE slipped 42%→32%** in FY26ttm — softer capital-market volumes + regulatory/fee drag. The toll-road is real; the traffic is sentiment-cyclical. **[H]**
- **BSE / MCX = violent operating leverage UP:** BSE NP nearly doubled (1,322→2,487), OPM 58%→64%, ROCE 47%→58%; MCX OPM 60%→71%, ROCE 43%→71% post-platform. Same fixed-cost rail, highest beta to volumes. **[M]**
- **CAMS = the steadiest grower:** AUM-linked (not trade-count-linked) revenue → OPM 45–47% and ROCE ~49–54% with the least drama. **[H]**
