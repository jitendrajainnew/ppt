## THE VARIANT VIEWS — where 80% of the return is  [CORE]

### Variant 1 — India's EV transition is *slower* than global (and that's mispriced both ways)
- **Consensus:** SUV-led volumes + Maruti dominance continue; EV steadily takes over and incumbents adapt smoothly.
- **Variant [M]:** India's transition is **slower than global** (charging gaps, affordability, hybrid bridge),
  so the **near-term winner is the SUV/hybrid-mix owner** (M&M, Maruti-via-Toyota-hybrids) — *but* the market
  **under-prices the Chinese-cost (BYD) threat at the affordable end** and treats **Maruti's scale moat as
  EV-proof when it's partly an ICE moat.** Own the funded mix-shifters; don't pay full price for the ICE-heavy
  scale leader as if the moat is structural.
- **Evidence:** EV penetration still ~2.5–3%; SUV mix >50% rising; hybrid demand strong; incumbents net-cash to
  fund the transition (Maruti, Eicher debt-free; M&M strong; Tata deleveraged interest ₹8,097cr→₹2,827cr ttm).
- **Falsified if:** EV penetration inflects **past ~10% of PVs materially faster** than the hybrid-bridge assumes.

### Variant 2 — the compounders (M&M/Eicher) beat the cyclicals (Maruti/Tata) *through* the cycle
- **Consensus:** All Indian auto names are one cyclical trade; ride the volume upcycle across the board.
- **Variant [H]:** The **real 10-yr data splits the set cleanly.** *Premiumisers* held far higher *trough* ROCE
  (M&M 11%, Eicher 17% at FY22) and **compounded margin through the cycle** (M&M OPM 12%→19%; Eicher 25–31%),
  while *mass-market/JLR-cyclicals* **swung with it** (Maruti ROCE 24%→6%→22%; Tata 9%→1%→20%→3%). *Own the
  premiumisation compounders across the cycle; trade the cyclicals only at trough ROCE.*
- **Evidence:** M&M net profit −₹321cr(FY20)→₹14,073cr(FY25); Eicher never below 17% ROCE; Maruti's FY22 6%
  ROCE was the buy signal that owned the FY24 snap to 24%; Tata's −₹28,724cr(FY19)→+₹31,807cr(FY24) turn.
- **Falsified if:** Maruti's margin *re-rates* structurally (OPM sustainably >15%) or M&M/Eicher margins
  *compress* through a down-cycle — i.e. the mix advantage stops translating into return stability.

### Variant 3 — Tata Motors (TMPV) is a JLR-beta turnaround, not a clean India-EV compounder
- **Consensus:** Tata = the India EV-PV winner; own it for the EV story.
- **Variant [M]:** The EV lead is real (rubric EV-readiness 5), **but the P&L is dominated by JLR's global
  cycle** — the **−₹28,724cr (FY19) → +₹82,645cr (FY26ttm)** swing and ROCE 1%→20%→3% are *JLR + leverage +
  demerger noise*, not steady India-PV economics. FY26ttm carries **₹86,113cr "other income"** (demerger/
  exceptional) lifting profit to ₹82,645cr — the clean operating read is **FY24–25 ROCE ~20%, OPM 13%**.
  *Size Tata as a high-beta turnaround/SOTP (PV + JLR + EV-arm optionality) on de-leveraging triggers — not as
  a stability compounder.*
- **Evidence:** Interest cost fell ₹8,097cr(FY21)→₹2,827cr(ttm) = deleveraging; FY19–FY22 = 4 straight loss
  years; FY26 OPM 6% / ROCE 3% vs FY24–25 13% / ~20%.
- **Falsified if:** the post-demerger India-PV+EV entity prints **sustained ~20% ROCE / 13% OPM ex-JLR-noise**
  — then it re-rates from turnaround to compounder.

## ★ FALSIFICATION TEST — the whole thesis is WRONG if  [CORE]
(from `four-wheelers.md` Module 15)
(a) **EV penetration inflects past ~10% of PVs materially faster** than the hybrid-bridge thesis assumes, **or**
(b) a **Chinese-cost BEV lands sub-₹10L at scale** in India (BYD vertical integration undercutting entry), **or**
(c) **Maruti's EV launch flops commercially** while Tata/BYD entrench the affordable EV end, **or**
(d) the **volume cycle rolls hard** — wholesales down + dealer inventory >50 days for 2+ quarters, **or**
(e) the **premiumisation split breaks** — M&M/Eicher margins compress through a down-cycle while Maruti's
re-rates (Variant 2 inverts).

## The one-line thesis  [CORE]
**Premiumisation is what compounds through a deeply cyclical, EV-threatened sector: own the rising-margin
compounders (M&M's SUV+farm mix at 12→19% OPM, Eicher's 30–53% ROCE premium moat) across the cycle; trade the
scale/JLR cyclicals (Maruti, Tata Motors/TMPV) only at trough ROCE; treat the EV inflection + Chinese-cost
entry as the falsifier; and avoid sub-scale ICE-only exposure.**

*Answers the Research Question: in India the ICE→EV transition is slower than global, so the near-term moat
**holds** for the SUV/hybrid-mix owner — but the structural EV moat is **genuinely unresolved**, so size the
incumbents to the cycle and watch the EV inflection as the thesis-killer. The decade's real winner in the data
is the premiumisation compounder (M&M), with Eicher the cleanest cross-cycle return moat.*
