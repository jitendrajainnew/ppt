## D. Granular data points from the financials (deep read)  [CORE]
- **Maruti:** OPM never re-rated over the decade (14%→13%); ROCE collapsed **24%→6% (FY22 chip/COVID trough)**
  then snapped back to 24% (FY24) — *the* textbook cyclical curve. Net cash, sub-zero working-capital days
  (cash conversion cycle −19 to −44). Profit ₹3,880cr (FY22) → ₹14,680cr (ttm) is a ~3.8× cycle recovery, not
  compounding. [S: Screener.in, ~June 2026]
- **M&M:** OPM **12%→19% (+7pts, monotonic FY20→FY26)**; net profit **−₹321cr (FY20 loss)** → ₹14,073cr (FY25)
  → ₹18,622cr (ttm) ≈ a multi-bagger off the trough; ROCE 7%→15% with no down-years since. The SUV mix-up
  (Thar/Scorpio-N/XUV) + farm-equipment franchise lifted margin *structurally*. **The decade's standout.** [S]
- **Eicher (RE):** ROCE **49–53% (FY17–19)**, never below 17% even at the FY22 trough, now ~30–31%; OPM parked
  **25–31%** ≈ 2× mass-market. Interest near-zero (debt-free), dividend payout rising to ~41%. The clearest
  capital-light premium-brand moat. [S]
- **Tata Motors (TMPV):** Net profit **−₹28,724cr (FY19)** (₹26.7k-cr+ JLR write-down era) through a 4-year
  loss streak (FY19–FY22) to **+₹31,807cr (FY24)** and **+₹82,645cr (FY26ttm)**; ROCE −0% (FY20) → 20% (FY25).
  Interest cost fell ₹8,097cr (FY21) → ₹2,827cr (FY26ttm) = **deleveraging**. JLR is the swing engine — high
  beta, biggest turnaround, lowest-quality earnings line (demerger noise in FY26). [S]
