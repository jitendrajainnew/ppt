*Maruti FY22 ₹3,880cr is the chip/COVID profit trough — less than half its FY18 peak. M&M's FY20
₹−321cr loss (a one-off tax/impairment + auto downturn) marks the bottom of its turnaround; it then
compounded to ₹14,073cr by FY25. Eicher's profit nearly **4×'d FY22→FY26ttm**.*
*‡ Tata Motors (TMPV) shows the **full JLR cycle**: a 4-year loss streak FY19–FY22 (bottom **−₹28,724cr
FY19**, the JLR write-down year; FY20 −₹11,975cr, FY21 −₹13,395cr, FY22 −₹11,309cr) → turnaround to
+₹31,807cr (FY24) → **+₹82,645cr (FY26ttm)**. The FY26ttm figure is **inflated by ₹86,113cr "other
income"** (demerger/exceptional) — the underlying FY24–25 ~₹28–32k-cr is the clean earnings power. This is
the single largest net-profit swing in the set: **−₹28,724cr → +₹82,645cr.***

## What the long-run data reveals

**1. Maruti is the textbook cyclical — the curve swings, it doesn't compound.**
- *Evidence:* ROCE 24% (FY17-18) → **6% (FY22)** → 24% (FY24) → 19% (TTM); net profit ₹7,881cr (FY18)
  → **₹3,880cr (FY22 trough)** → ₹14,500cr (FY25). OPM essentially flat over the decade (14%→13%).
- *Interpretation:* Returns track the volume/commodity/chip cycle, not a widening moat. Margins never
  re-rated despite ~12% sales CAGR — classic mass-market price-taker economics.
- *Conclusion:* A **buy-at-trough cyclical**, not a steady compounder. The FY22 ROCE of 6% was the
  signal — bought there, you owned the FY24 recovery to 24%. **[H]**

**2. M&M is the standout compounder of the decade — premiumisation made visible.**
- *Evidence:* OPM **12% → 19%** (+7 points, monotonic rise FY20→FY26); ROCE 7% (FY20) → 15% (TTM)
  with no down-years since; net profit ₹−321cr (FY20 loss) → **₹14,073cr (FY25)** → ₹18,622cr TTM.
- *Interpretation:* The SUV mix-up (XUV/Thar/Scorpio-N) plus a high-return farm-equipment franchise
  lifted margins *structurally* — this is the premiumisation thesis paying out in the P&L, not a
  cyclical bounce. Improvement persists through the same macro Maruti swung in.
- *Conclusion:* **The standout compounder of the decade** among the four-wheeler incumbents — rising
  margin AND rising ROCE AND ~6× profit growth off the trough. **[H]**

**3. Eicher (Royal Enfield) is the premium-brand moat in one ratio line.**
- *Evidence:* ROCE **49-53% (FY17-19)**, never below 17% even at the FY22 trough, now ~30-31%; OPM
  parked at 25-31% — roughly double mass-market four-wheeler margins.
- *Interpretation:* A premium-brand franchise earns capital-light, pricing-power returns the volume
  OEMs cannot. The step-down from 50%+ to ~30% reflects capacity capex + competition (not erosion of
  the brand), and 30% ROCE is still elite.
- *Conclusion:* The clearest **premium-brand moat** in the set — structurally higher, more *stable*
  returns through the cycle. **[H]**

**4. Tata Motors (TMPV) is the deep-cyclical, JLR-driven turnaround — the highest beta in the set.**
- *Evidence:* Net profit **−₹28,724cr (FY19)** through a 4-year loss streak (FY19–FY22) → +₹31,807cr
  (FY24) → **+₹82,645cr (FY26ttm)**; ROCE 9% (FY18) → **−0% (FY20) / 1% (FY22)** → ~20% (FY24-25) → 3%
  (FY26ttm, demerger-distorted). Interest cost fell ₹8,097cr (FY21) → ₹2,827cr (ttm) = real deleveraging.
- *Interpretation:* The P&L is dominated by **JLR's global premium cycle + leverage**, not steady India-PV
  economics — bigger swings, bigger write-downs (FY19), bigger recovery than any peer. The FY26ttm spike is
  partly **₹86,113cr exceptional/other income** (demerger), not operating earnings; the clean read is the
  FY24-25 ~20% ROCE / 13% OPM.
- *Conclusion:* A **buy-on-deleveraging high-beta turnaround / SOTP** (PV + JLR + EV-arm optionality), and
  the listed **EV-PV leader** — *not* a stability compounder. Highest reward, highest risk in the set. **[M]**

**5. The sector is cyclical, but premiumisation is what compounds through it.**
- *Evidence:* All three troughed FY20-22 (chip shortage + COVID). But the *premiumisers* (M&M's SUV/
  farm mix, Eicher's RE) held far higher trough ROCE (11% / 17%) and exited stronger, while mass-market
  Maruti's ROCE collapsed to 6% and merely *recovered* to its old range.
- *Interpretation:* Cyclicality is the sector's base rate; the differentiator is mix. Premium/SUV
  winners compounded *through* the cycle; the mass-market name swung *with* it.
- *Conclusion:* Tie-out to `four-wheelers.md`: **buy the mass-market cyclicals at trough ROCE
  (Maruti's FY22 = 6%), but own the premiumisation/SUV compounders (M&M, Eicher) across the cycle.**
  The decade's standout is M&M. **[M]** — *contingent on the EV-transition risk below.*

**5. Caveats — the EV transition and the missing giant.**
- *Evidence:* The 10-year tables predate mass-EV penetration; today's incumbent ROCE/OPM reflect the
  ICE/hybrid franchise. Tata Motors — the EV-PV leader and largest group by revenue — is **absent**
  (demerger data gap).
- *Interpretation:* The premium/SUV moats above are an **incumbent-ICE** story; EV transition is the
  forward risk to exactly these returns, and the one listed EV-PV leader is missing from the sample.
- *Conclusion:* Treat the conclusions as **incumbent-incumbent**, not whole-sector; size EV-transition
  risk separately and re-pull Tata Motors once the post-demerger series stabilises. **[L]**
