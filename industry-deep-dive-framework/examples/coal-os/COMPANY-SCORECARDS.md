## The rubric (coal-specific weights, derived from `coal.md` Module 11)

> `coal.md`'s Module 11 weights a coal business on cost-curve, volume/realisation mix, balance
> sheet/dividend coverage, transition resilience, logistics, and capital allocation. We collapse
> that into six scoring factors tuned to what the **10-year financials can actually evidence** —
> the cash-generation (ROCE) and payout columns are the heart of the coal call.

| Factor | Weight | "5/5" |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost-curve / reserve position** | 25% | Low-cost opencast, pithead, deep reserve base — survives any price |
| **Cash generation / ROCE** | 20% | Sustained franchise-level ROCE (30%+), high margin through the cycle |
| **Capital allocation / payout** | 20% | Returns cash from a shrinking-runway asset; high, covered payout; no empire-building |
| **Volume-growth runway** | 15% | Secure FSA base + e-auction/coking upside; production ramping ahead of target |
| **Regulatory / ESG resilience** | 10% | Hedged transition (RE pivot), low stranded-asset / cess exposure |
| **Balance sheet** | 10% | Net cash / low leverage, self-funds growth |

**Scale:** 1 red flag · 3 average · 5 best-in-class.

## Per-factor scoring with reasoning (from the 10-yr data)

### Cost-curve / reserve position (25%)
- **Coal India = 5.** ~80% of national output, low-cost opencast, unmatched reserve base — the moat.
  The 35–107% ROCE *is* the cost-curve advantage showing up in returns. **[H]**
- **NTPC = 3.** Med-cost captive mining, own-use; the genco economics are regulated, not cost-curve-driven. **[M]**
- **NLC India = 3.** Med-cost lignite; the power leg dilutes mining economics. **[M]**

### Cash generation / ROCE (20%)
- **Coal India = 5.** A decade of **35–107% ROCE** and ~33% peak OPM — franchise-monopoly returns,
  not utility returns. *Flag:* FY26ttm fell to **35% ROCE / 24% OPM** — best-in-class but softening. **[H]**
- **NTPC = 3.** ROCE pinned **~8–11% every year** — assured-RoE by regulatory design, low variance. **[H]**
- **NLC India = 2.** ROCE **~7–13%, just 7% in FY24**; lumpy profit (FY16 NP collapsed to ₹68cr). The
  lowest-return cash engine of the three. **[H]**

### Capital allocation / payout (20%)
- **Coal India = 5.** Paid out **95–146% in FY15–18**, ~42–53% recently — textbook cash-cow harvest of a
  shrinking-runway asset; modest capex. Correct policy for the asset type. **[H]**
- **NTPC = 4.** Steady **~32–44% payout** while funding a large regulated + RE capex book — disciplined
  compounder, the hedged play. **[H]**
- **NLC India = 3.** Payout **down to 15%** (FY26ttm) — retaining for the lignite+RE build; reasonable but
  not a cash-return story (and the one wild FY16 588% print was a tax artefact). **[M]**

### Volume-growth runway (15%)
- **Coal India = 4.** Secure FSA base + e-auction/coking upside, ramping toward ~1bn-t; terminal risk caps it. **[M]**
- **NTPC = 4.** Power-demand growth + backward captive-mining ramp + RE build = long runway. **[M]**
- **NLC India = 3.** Integrated lignite-to-power, modest growth; sales 9.7% 10-yr CAGR but small base. **[M]**

### Regulatory / ESG resilience (10%)
- **NTPC = 5.** Most hedged — large RE/nuclear pivot, pass-through genco, lowest stranded-asset risk. **[M]**
- **NLC India = 4.** Lignite + growing RE, regulated integrated — partially hedged. **[M]**
- **Coal India = 3.** Pure coal; diversification (solar/CBM) nascent; cess + FSA cap + 2070 ceiling = the
  terminal risk. The franchise's one structural weak spot. **[M]**

### Balance sheet (10%)
- **Coal India = 5.** Near **net cash**, tiny interest (~₹1,216cr on ₹168k cr sales) — funds payout + capex easily. **[H]**
- **NLC India = 3.** Levered (interest ~₹1,222cr on a far smaller base) — build-phase leverage. **[M]**
- **NTPC = 3.** Capex-levered (**interest ~₹13,801cr**) by the regulated-utility model — serviceable but heavy. **[M]**

## What the REAL data establishes (and the one flag)

1. **Coal India is the runaway #1 — and it is the cash machine.** A decade of **35–107% ROCE**, ~33% peak
   OPM, net profit 5.3×'d ₹7,038cr→₹37,369cr, ~net cash, high payout. These are franchise-monopoly economics
   hard to reconcile with a terminal-decline multiple. **[H]**
2. **⚠️ FLAG — Coal India's recent softening.** FY25→FY26ttm is the first real crack: **ROCE 48%→35%, OPM
   33%→24%, net profit ₹35,302cr→₹31,071cr.** Exactly the melting-ice-cube shape — early, maybe cyclical,
   but the FY26 full-year print is now the decisive data point. Does **not** dethrone it, but caps the score. **[M]**
3. **NTPC is the steady regulated anchor (#2).** ROCE **~8–11% every single year**, profit compounding
   relentlessly, lowest variance, best transition hedge (RE pivot) — bond-like, not a cash cow. **[H]**
4. **NLC India is the lower-return hybrid (#3).** ROCE **~7–13%**, lumpy, payout cut to 15% — lignite bolted
   to regulated power dilutes the mining economics. **Not a Coal India proxy.** **[H]**

## Re-grade triggers
- **Coal India:** FY26 full-year ROCE/OPM **stabilising** (rollover was cyclical) holds score; a **second**
  down-year or a dividend cut would pull Cap-Alloc/ROCE and the rank narrative toward "value trap."
- **NTPC:** RE-arm (NTPC Green) scaling + ROCE drifting above ~11% lifts ROCE 3→4 → score → ~3.75.
- **NLC India:** RE build-out lifting blended ROCE toward double digits sustainably lifts ROCE 2→3 → score → ~3.10.
