## THE VARIANT VIEWS — where 80% of the return is

### Variant 1 — "melting ice cube" vs **"cash cow for longer"** (the core debate)
- **Consensus:** Coal = melting ice cube; terminal decline; cheap-for-a-reason value trap.
- **Variant [M]:** **For *India specifically*, a multi-decade baseload cash cow** — demand grows into the
  mid-2030s on energy security, ESG capital-starvation tightens supply for incumbents who *can* still raise
  capital, and the **~6–8% dividend is more durable than the discount implies.**
- **Evidence (the REAL data):** Coal India ran **ROCE 35–107% across FY15–26** and ~33% peak OPM — *franchise-
  monopoly, not utility, returns sustained through a full decade and commodity cycles.* That durability is
  hard to reconcile with a market pricing it as terminal-decline. **[H]**
- **Own:** the low-cost, net-cash, high-payout near-monopoly (Coal India) for the yield + cycle. **Avoid:**
  leveraged merchant thermal exposure.

### Variant 2 — the bears just got their *first* real evidence (and it's why the test matters)
- **Consensus (bull complacency):** the cash machine is forever.
- **Variant [M]:** **FY25→FY26ttm is the first genuine crack** — ROCE **48%→35%**, OPM **33%→24%**, net profit
  **₹35,302cr→₹31,071cr (TTM)**. This is *exactly* the shape the terminal-decline camp predicted: realisation /
  volume softening compressing returns from the top. Early and possibly cyclical (e-auction premia, wage
  revision, one bad year) — but **directionally the melting-ice-cube signature.** Not decisive; "watch closely."
- **Evidence:** the 10-yr P&L/ratios series — same period ROCE 48%→35% *and* OPM 33%→24% move together.
- **Net read:** cash-cow thesis still leads on the weight of evidence + the dividend runway, but the
  falsification test is now **partially triggered.** **[M]**

### Variant 3 — Coal India ≠ NLC ≠ NTPC (don't buy the wrong "coal" exposure)
- **Consensus:** PSU coal/power names are interchangeable energy-security plays.
- **Variant [H]:** The REAL data says they are three different businesses. **Coal India** = 35–107% ROCE pure-
  play monopoly cash cow. **NTPC** = ROCE ~8–11% *every year*, bond-like regulated compounder, best transition
  hedge — buy for predictability + capacity growth, *not* coal cash economics. **NLC India** = ROCE ~7–13%,
  lumpy (FY16 NP ₹68cr), payout cut to 15% — lignite bolted to regulated power *dilutes* the mining economics.
- **Net:** if you want the coal *cash machine*, that is Coal India alone — and you accept the terminal risk.

## ★ THE FALSIFICATION TEST (from `coal.md` Module 15)

> The cash-cow variant is **proven WRONG** if any of:
> - **(a)** India coal **demand posts back-to-back YoY declines**, or
> - **(b)** **utility-scale storage LCOE collapses** enough to displace baseload and **thermal PLF rolls over**, or
> - **(c)** a **domestic carbon levy / sharp cess hike** compresses realisations, or
> - **(d)** **Coal India cuts the dividend** / payout guidance falls materially.

**Where the test stands on the REAL data:** the 10-year series delivers **both signals at once** — a decade
of exceptional, durable returns (cash-cow) **and** a clear FY25→FY26ttm rollover in ROCE/OPM/net profit (the
first decline evidence). **The cash-cow read still leads, but the test is now partially triggered** —
**CIL's FY26 full-year ROCE/OPM is the next decisive data point.** Payout held at 53% (FY26ttm), so trigger
(d) has *not* fired. **[M]**

evidence, so monitor the FY26 print as the decisive test, not a 1–3y thesis-killer.**
