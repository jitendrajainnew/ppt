## "What the 10-yr data says" — per company  [CORE]

### 1. JSPL (Jindal Steel) — the turnaround, now the highest raw score  **[M]**
- **The data:** loss-making **every year FY15–FY20** (ROCE −1% to 5%, EPS as low as −₹32), then turned
  hard: **+₹6,766cr profit and 24% ROCE in FY22**, and OPM has held the **highest trough floor of the
  four (≈18–20%)** since. Interest fell sharply as it deleveraged (steel.md: ₹3,866cr FY18 → ₹1,294cr
  FY24). **[M]**
- **Scoring:** C2 balance-sheet = **5** (the deleveraging is *in the data*, not a single good year);
  C1 = 4 (med–high captive ore `[verify]`); C3 = 3 (mix improving — plates/rails — not yet auto-grade);
  C4 = 4 (deleveraged through the trough, expanding into recovery). **The classic "balance-sheet
  repair → re-rate" arc.** *Watch:* that the post-FY22 margin floor holds. **[M]**

### 2. JSW Steel — best operator, the lowest-regret cyclical hold  **[H]**
- **The data:** the **shallowest trough ROCE (5% FY16)** vs negative for SAIL/JSPL; **only one loss
  year (FY16, −₹481cr, marginal)**; **OPM never fell below ~13–16%**; *and* it **compounded sales
  fastest (~12.3% CAGR, ₹53k→₹185k cr)**. Net profit **₹25,508cr FY26ttm** — the highest of the
  four. The efficient, low-conversion-cost operator earns the cycle's premium and prints the mildest
  drawdowns. **[H]**
- **Scoring:** C3 VAP mix = **5** (auto/coated franchise, the margin moat); C4 = 4, C5 = 4 (IBC
  discipline — Bhushan Power, Monnet bought cheap). C1 = **3** — the one structural gap: **ore-short
  (≈15–20% captive)**, gives back ≈₹2,000–4,000/t to captive peers at the peak `[verify]`. C2 = 3
  (≈2–3x net-debt/EBITDA on growth capex `[verify]`). **If you must hold steel through a full cycle,
  this is the name.** **[H]**

### 3. Tata Steel — highest cyclical beta; own for the trough trade, not the hold  **[H]**
- **The data:** the **textbook capital cycle in one row** — net profit **−₹4,169cr (FY17) →
  +₹41,749cr (FY22 peak) → −₹4,910cr (FY24)**; ROCE **1% → 31% → 7%**. **Four loss years (FY15–17,
  FY24)**. OPM **more than halved peak-to-trough (26%→10%)**. Maximum torque to the commodity price.
  **[H]**
- **Scoring:** C1 cost-curve = **5** (captive iron ore — India's best-positioned on ore) and C6 = 4
  (most advanced decarb/CBAM positioning); C3 = 4 (auto-grade). But C2 = 3 and C4 = 3 — the **Europe
  drag** (the Corus value-destruction legacy) and the FY24 loss cap it. **Devastating if bought at
  peak earnings (FY22), explosive if bought into the FY24 loss.** Raw score is actually highest on
  ore, but the cyclical-beta and Europe overhang make it a *timing* name. **[H]**

### 4. SAIL — captive ore, but commodity-tilted with the deepest trough  **[H]**
- **The data:** the **deepest trough of the four (ROCE −6% FY16)**, three straight loss years
  (FY16–18), **OPM halved peak-to-trough (21%→11%)** and a **low absolute margin floor** — the
  commodity-tilted PSU profile. FY26ttm net profit ₹3,373cr on ₹110,811cr sales (thin). **[H]**
- **Scoring:** C1 = 4 (captive own-mine ore — a genuine cost-curve asset); but C3 VAP = **2**
  (commodity-tilted, lowest value-added mix), C4 = 2 (PSU capacity-timing discipline weakest),
  C6 = 2 (commodity/high-carbon profile). **Captive ore can't rescue a commodity mix and weak
  capital-timing discipline** — the lowest weighted score (**2.95**). **[H]**

## ⚠️ What grounding in the REAL data CHANGED (vs the illustrative steel.md scores)  [CORE]

1. **JSPL is the highest raw score — the deleveraging is confirmed, not a one-year fluke.** The
   6-straight-loss-years → re-rate arc and the **highest trough OPM floor (≈20%)** are *in the
   series*. Balance-sheet C2 = 5 is earned. **[M]**
2. **JSW's "best operator" claim is fully vindicated by the data** — shallowest trough ROCE, only one
   (marginal) loss year, fastest sales CAGR. The *only* thing holding it off #1 on the rubric is the
   structural **ore-short** C1=3. The data says: **steadiest hold, not highest cost-curve score.** **[H]**
3. **Tata & SAIL are confirmed high-beta trough-trades, not holds.** Both swing to outright losses at
   the bottom and mega-profits at the top; OPM halves peak-to-trough. Tata's captive-ore C1=5 gives it
   the highest *raw* score, but the loss years make it a **timing** name. **[H]**
4. **No structural compounder exists here.** Even JSW's ROCE ran **5% → 28% → 8%**. Steel rewards
   *entry discipline over quality-and-hold* — the scorecard ranks *operators within a cycle*, it does
   not crown a buy-and-forget compounder. **[H]**

## Re-grade triggers  [APPENDIX]
- **JSW captive-ore block win** → C1 3→4 → weighted ~3.85 → could take #1 (closes its one gap).
- **JSPL margin floor slips below ~15%** for 2+ quarters → C2/C3 cut → turnaround thesis weakens.
- **Tata Europe restructured / FY-loss not repeated** → C2/C4 up → its ore advantage (raw #1) surfaces.
- **SAIL value-added mix or net-debt discipline improves** → C3/C4 up from the floor.
