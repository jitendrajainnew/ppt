## What to watch next (cadence)  [CORE]
1. **Weekly:** HRC + iron-ore + coking-coal prices → recompute the **steel spread** (the trade signal).
2. **Monthly:** China steel-export MT (is the flood worsening?) + India production/utilisation (JPC).
3. **Quarterly:** EBITDA/tonne, net-debt/EBITDA, value-added mix, capex progress (esp. JSW ≈38 MTPA ramp).
4. **Event-driven:** ore-block auctions (cost-curve self-help, esp. JSW), safeguard/anti-dumping duties,
   CBAM milestones (2026+).
5. **The single number:** if the **steel spread compresses to/under trough for 4+ quarters with no
   safeguard response**, the bear case (steel.md §15 falsification) is live. **[H]**
