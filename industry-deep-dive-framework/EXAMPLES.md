## What the differences teach you

The framework is fixed, but **emphasis flexes with the industry's nature**:

- **Cement (mature, cyclical, domestic):** heaviest on **Module 10 (evaluation scorecard)**,
  **Module 11 (tiering players A/B/C)**, and **replacement-cost valuation**. History and
  consolidation matter because the structure *is* the story.

- **Dredging (niche oligopoly services):** heaviest on **Module 6 (Porter's, who builds the
  ships)** and **company-level balance-sheet repair / triggers**. Small universe → every
  player gets a full dissection.

- **Memory (global tech supercycle):** heaviest on **Module 1/8 (the macro AI driver + the
  supply-demand supercycle)** and **Module 4 (segments: HBM/NAND/HDD/HBF)**. The *cycle* is
  the thesis; history is almost irrelevant.

- **Green Energy (multi-sub-sector growth):** the **most complete** — it ran the *entire
  spine separately for each sub-sector* (solar, hydrogen, battery, wind) and produced the
  best **Module 5 value-chain margin map** and **Module 7 geography** and **Module 13 sources**.

**Rule of thumb for a new industry:** identify its nature first (mature-cyclical /
niche-oligopoly / tech-supercycle / structural-growth), then **weight the modules**
accordingly — but still *consider every module*.
