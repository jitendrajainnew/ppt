# Industry Archetypes — Default Module Weightings

> Not every industry needs equal depth on every module. Pick the archetype first; it tells
> you which modules to over-invest in and which to keep light. This makes research **faster**
> (you stop spending equal time on modules that don't move the answer) and **sharper** (you
> go deep where this industry's value and risk actually concentrate).

Pick one (or a hybrid) before you start the TEMPLATE.

| Archetype | Examples | What drives returns | **Over-weight these modules** | Keep lighter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Commodity / Cyclical** | Steel, Cement, Shipping, Chemicals, Paper | Where you are in the **cycle** + the **cost curve** | **8 Cycle + Dashboard**, 5 Value chain, 6 Supply/structure, 16 Valuation (replacement cost) | 3 Tech, 15 Contrarian (often obvious) |
| **Technology / Innovation** | Memory, Semis, Software, EVs | **Product cycles + innovation** staying ahead | **3 How-it's-made/next-gen**, 4 Segments, 8 Demand supercycle, 15 Contrarian | 2 History, 9 Policy (usually) |
| **Regulated** | Pharma, Banks, Insurance, Utilities | **Policy + compliance + capital** | **9 Regulation** (lead actor), 10 Capital allocation, 13/14 Failure & risk, 12 Company (compliance) | 7 Geography (sometimes) |
| **Infrastructure / Asset-heavy** | Power, Ports, Airports, Roads, Dredging | **Capital intensity + asset utilisation + policy** | **10 Capital allocation**, 8 Utilisation/demand, 9 Policy, 16 Valuation (asset/DCF) | 3 Tech, 4 Segments |
| **Network / Platform** | Exchanges, Payment networks, Marketplaces, Credit bureaus, Telecom towers | **Network effects + switching costs + winner-take-most** | **6 Structure** (concentration, moats), 5 Value chain (take-rate), 8b KPI Dashboard (share/engagement), 15 Contrarian | 3 How-it's-made, 9 Policy (varies) |

> **Why Network/Platform is its own archetype:** its economics don't fit the other four. Value
> compounds with scale (each user makes the product more valuable), moats are switching costs
> and data, and markets tip to one or two winners. The right lens is *share of the network and
> the durability of the moat*, not the cost curve or the asset base.

## How to use the weighting

1. **Tag your archetype** at the top of the TEMPLATE.
2. **Spend ~60% of your effort** on the over-weighted modules for that archetype.
3. Still **complete every module** (the CHECKLIST minimum) — weighting changes *depth*, not
   *coverage*. A regulated-industry write-up that skips the value chain is still incomplete.
4. **Hybrids are common.** Green Energy = Technology (solar tech) × Infrastructure (plant
   operators) × Regulated (subsidies). Pharma = Regulated × Technology (R&D pipeline). When
   hybrid, take the union of the over-weighted modules.

## Mandatory deliverables per archetype (prescriptive, not just "spend more time")

Each archetype *must* produce these specific artifacts — if they're missing, the deep dive is
incomplete for that archetype, regardless of page count:

| Archetype | Must include |
|---|---|
| **Commodity / Cyclical** | Cost curve · capacity-addition pipeline · utilisation trend · Cycle Dashboard · replacement-cost valuation |
| **Technology / Innovation** | Technology roadmap · product roadmap · innovation/obsolescence risk · next-gen-shift call |
| **Regulated** | Regulatory timeline (approvals/rulings ahead) · compliance scorecard per company · policy-reversal risk |
| **Infrastructure / Asset-heavy** | Asset-utilisation analysis · project IRRs · capital-allocation deep dive · funding/leverage map |
| **Network / Platform** | Network-effect & switching-cost analysis · market-share/concentration trend · take-rate economics · winner-take-most assessment |

## Why this matters (the per-deck evidence)

The four source decks each *implicitly* leaned on their archetype's dominant module — this
doc just makes that explicit and reusable:

| Deck | Archetype | Dominant module it leaned on |
|---|---|---|
| Cement | Commodity | Structure + consolidation (pricing power) |
| Dredging | Infrastructure | Policy + capital (fleet, balance-sheet repair) |
| Memory | Technology | Cycles (the AI/HBM supercycle) |
| Green Energy | Hybrid (Tech×Infra×Regulated) | Value chain + geography + policy |

**Rule of thumb:** if you can't name your archetype, you don't yet understand what drives
returns in this industry — and that *is* the Research Question.
